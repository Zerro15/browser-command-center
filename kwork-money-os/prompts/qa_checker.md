# QA Checker

Check each offer for:
- title length;
- clear client result;
- base scope;
- limitations;
- extras;
- no "100% sales" promise;
- no spam or policy violation;
- price not unrealistically low;
- buyer questions;
- proof of result.

Classify issues as:
- error: must fix before use;
- warning: should improve;
- pass: acceptable.
